EYE ON GAMBIA is a community-based youth organization building leaders through education, volunteering and community development across The Gambia.

Join or volunteerSee our work

Who we are

We are a youth-led organization rooted in our communities. We believe young Gambians are not only the future of the country, they are already shaping it today.

Our mission: to empower young people to lead, learn and serve, so that every community grows stronger, cleaner and more united.

What we aim to do

  • Develop confident youth leaders who act for their communities.
  • Support students with learning activities and better access to education.
  • Encourage volunteering as a habit of service and unity.
  • Protect the environment through cleaning and tree planting.
  • Build partnerships that bring lasting change to local communities.

What we do

Our work is practical and hands-on. Here is where we show up.

Education support

Learning activities that help students build knowledge, confidence and a love of learning.

School excursions

Trips that take students beyond the classroom to see, explore and learn from the world around them.

Community cleaning

Neighbours and volunteers working side by side to keep our streets, markets and schools clean.

Tree planting

Planting trees for shade, food and a greener future for the next generation.

Youth leadership

Opportunities for young people to organize, speak up and lead projects that matter.

Volunteering

An open door for anyone who wants to give time and skills to their community.

Recognized by schools we work with

Jambanjelly Upper and Senior Secondary School gave EYE ON GAMBIA a Certificate of Appreciation. It thanks our president for helping make the 2025 nationwide school excursion a success.

This is the kind of trust we want to keep earning, one project at a time.
